# Music credits

Both tracks are **CC0** (public domain dedication): free to use, modify and sell, with no
attribution required. They are credited here anyway, as the authors asked to be appreciated.

| File | Track | Author | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `title.mp3` | A Legend Will Rise (Orchestral) | CodeManu | https://opengameart.org/content/a-legend-will-rise-orchestral | CC0 1.0 |
| `battle.wav` | Epic Boss Battle [Seamlessly Looping] | Juhani Junkala (SubspaceAudio) | https://opengameart.org/content/boss-battle-music | CC0 1.0 |

Both replaced commercial soundtracks the Unity version used (Dawn of War and Castle
Crashers), which could not ship in a public release. Both are set to loop in their import
settings; the battle track is authored to loop seamlessly.

Before adding any new music or sound, confirm its licence on the track's own page. Prefer
CC0. If it is CC-BY, add the required credit here and in the game's credits.
